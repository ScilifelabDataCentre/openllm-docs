# SciLifeLab OpenLLM Policy

SciLifeLab OpenLLM is a pilot project where we are currently assessing the technical and infrastructure setup. We currently do not have a use policy because we have not yet settled on the technical setup and have not yet carried out assessments required to establish expected confidentiality, integrity, and availability of our service and user content. Keep in mind that we do not guarantee that this pilot will become a production grade service at this point. We will create and publish a clear use policy and describe expectations once it is possible and before we move on to offering a production grade service.

In the meantime, please keep in mind that this service is for testing purposes and:

- Do not send any text, data, or code that requires any level of confidentiality above publicly available information (in other words, do not send things that are not public information or where the information would not be allowed to be made public);
- Do not expect any guaranteed integrity, uptime, latency, or throughput; Keep in mind that this is a pilot and it can be shut down at any time;
- Do not use the service for anything that violates the requirements or policies from your own university (employer), the Swedish law, the EU regulations, SciLifeLab policies, ethical review board or research ethics committee decisions, etc.
