# Creating an account

## Who is eligible to create an account

The pilot of SciLifeLab OpenLLM is available to staff employed at all units (across all platforms) within SciLifeLab as well as members of research groups affiliated with SciLifeLab. The latter includes, for example, DDLS fellows and members of their groups, SciLifeLab fellows and members of their groups, PULSE postdocs, SciLifeLab group leaders. When working on projects with Swedish or international collaborators we allow the collaborators to also create an account. The goal of the pilot is to learn and assess the capacity, so while we welcome those belonging to the mentioned groups to register and use the service, we cannot guarantee that we will have enough room to accept everyone. For this reason, each created account is manually approved before access to the service is granted.

## Registration process

In order to start using the service, you need to fill out the registration form. Please register with your official work email to register an account. Once you submit your information, you should receive an email with a confirmation link. Follow the link to verify your email. After this, someone from the SciLifeLab OpenLLM team will review your information, and if there is enough room to accept another user we will approve your registration. At that point you will receive an email informing you that you can now log in and use the service.
